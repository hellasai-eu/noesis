# Terms of Service

**Last updated:** 2026-09-15
**Version:** 1.3 (draft)

## 1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between **Hellenic Institute for AI & Education** ("Noesis",
"we") and you, the person using the platform.

**Your school's contract governs the service itself.** If you are a pupil, a
parent or a teacher, your access comes through your school's agreement with us,
and where these terms and that agreement differ, the school's agreement wins
for anything about the service. These terms cover your own use of it.

## 2. Accounts

- Accounts are created by your school, or by invitation from it. You cannot
  sign yourself up to someone else's institution.
- **Keep your password to yourself.** Passwords must be at least eight
  characters with letters and digits, and known-leaked passwords are refused.
  Two-factor authentication is available in your account menu and worth
  enabling — and do not reuse a password from elsewhere.
- Tell your school immediately if you think someone else has used your account.
- We record sign-ins, including IP address, for security. See the Privacy Policy.

## 3. What you may use the platform for

Learning and teaching, as your school directs. Specifically, you agree not to:

- Try to reach another pupil's, class's or school's data.
- Probe, scan, or attempt to break the platform's security.
- Automate access, scrape content, or use the AI features to generate content
  unrelated to your studies.
- Upload material you have no right to upload, or that is unlawful, abusive or
  harmful.
- Use the tutors to produce content that is harmful, or to circumvent the
  safety screening.

**What you send is screened, and so is what comes back.** Your message to a
tutor is checked for harmful material, and so is the tutor's reply. A flag
pauses the session and tells your school, and flagged content — yours or the
tutor's — is retained for review by your school.

Be clear about what that check can and cannot do. The tutor writes to your
screen as it goes, so **its reply is checked after you have read it, and a
flagged reply is not taken away**; it is recorded, the session pauses, and your
school is told. Your own message is checked at the same moment the tutor is
asked rather than before, so the reply is held back briefly while the check
runs — usually long enough to stop a flagged message before any of the reply
appears, but not always. The check is automated, it can miss things, and when
it is unavailable a reply is delivered unchecked rather than the tutor
stopping. If a tutor says something wrong or upsetting, tell your teacher.

**Tutor conversations pause for your teacher.** After a set number of messages
a session stops and waits for a teacher to look at it and release it. The
tutoring screen tells you how many messages are left before that happens.

  models on it.
- **Your work stays yours**, and forms part of your school's educational record.
- **The platform is ours.** These terms give you the right to use it, not to
  copy, resell or reverse-engineer it.
- **AI-generated content** — questions, summaries, study guides — is generated
  for your school from your school's material and is your school's to use for
  teaching. We make no claim that such content is original, and no claim that
  it is correct.

## 5. The AI, and what we do not promise about it

This is the clause worth reading twice.

**The AI makes mistakes.** It misreads unusual phrasing, and it can state
something confidently that is not true. Every AI output shown to a pupil is
labelled as AI-produced.

**A teacher decides.** Your grades on written answers are your teacher's own —
since 14 September 2026 the AI assigns none, and instead drafts a private note your
teacher may use, change or ignore. An AI evaluation is a proposal; your
teacher can replace it, and their judgement is the one that counts. **Do not
treat an AI explanation as authority.**

**AI output is formative, and only formative.** AI-generated assessments and
feedback are provided for formative learning purposes only.
They are **not intended to determine official grades, admission, promotion,
educational placement, or any other consequential educational decision**, and
must not be used that way. Educators remain responsible for formal assessment.
This is a term of the school's agreement with us, not a suggestion.

**We do not warrant** that AI-generated questions are free of error, that grades
match a human marker's, or that generated study material is complete or
accurate. We have not yet measured grading accuracy — the AI notice says so.
